Event Overview

Our Annual Community Dinner is a beloved tradition that brings together church members and community neighbors for an evening of warmth, fellowship, and celebration. This event showcases the heart of our church - bringing people together in love and unity.

The dinner features delicious homemade food, live music from our worship team, activities for children, and an opportunity for everyone to connect and build meaningful relationships. It's a wonderful expression of our church family coming together to serve and celebrate.

The Vision

Our goal was to create an event that would bring the entire community together, breaking down barriers and building relationships across all ages and backgrounds.

The Impact

The dinner was a tremendous success, with over 300 attendees. We raised funds for local families in need and strengthened the bonds within our church community.
