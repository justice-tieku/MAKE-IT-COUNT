Frequently Asked Questions

Church FAQs

Common questions about our church and what to expect

Need Assistance?

Have more questions? We'd love to help. Reach out to us and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

Connect with Us

What are your service times?

Our Sunday worship service is at 10:00 AM every week. We also have midweek Bible study on Wednesday evenings at 7:00 PM and youth group on Friday nights at 6:30 PM.

Where are you located?

We are located in the University of Ghana, Sefmat Catering services

What should I wear?

Come as you are! We have a casual and welcoming atmosphere. You'll see people in everything from jeans to dress shirts. We care more about your heart than your outfit.

Do you have programs for children?

Yes! We offer children's ministry during our Sunday service for ages 0-12, as well as Vacation Bible School in the summer and special events throughout the year.

How can I become a member?

We offer a membership class every quarter. Contact our church office for the next available date and we'll guide you through the process of becoming a part of our church family.

What happens during a service?

Our service typically includes worship through music, a prayer time, a message from the Bible, and an opportunity to respond. The entire service lasts about 75 minutes.

Will I be asked to give money?

We do have an offering time during our service, but it is completely optional. As a guest, you are not expected to give. We are grateful for your presence, not your money.

How can I get connected?

We have a Connect Corner in the lobby after every service where you can learn about upcoming events, small groups, and volunteer opportunities. Our greeters would love to help you get connected.

Are there membership requirements?

We ask that members agree with our statement of faith, have a personal relationship with Jesus Christ, and be active in the life of the church. We also ask that you complete our membership class.

Can I start serving right away?

We'd love to have you serve! We recommend attending our church for a few weeks to get to know us, then connect with a ministry leader to explore serving opportunities that match your gifts.
