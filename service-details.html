Sunday Service

Join us every Sunday for uplifting worship, an inspiring message, and fellowship with the church family. Come as you are and experience the love of God in community.

Age-Appropriate Teaching

Our youth ministry offers programs tailored to different age groups, ensuring that every child and teenager receives relevant, age-appropriate biblical teaching.

Fun & Engaging

We believe learning about faith should be enjoyable! Through games, music, and creative activities, we make Bible stories come alive.

Mentorship

Each youth is paired with a caring adult mentor who provides guidance, support, and encouragement throughout their faith journey.

Safe Environment

We prioritize safety with background-checked volunteers, secure check-in systems, and a culture of transparency and accountability.

Our Weekly Activities

Our youth ministry meets every Friday evening for games, worship, Bible study, and small group discussions. We also have special events throughout the year including retreats, service projects, and summer camp.

Our goal is to create a space where teenagers feel accepted, valued, and excited about growing in their faith alongside friends and mentors.
